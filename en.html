  • AI Is Literature: Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Literature

  • Robots Writing Novels: Literature in the Age of AI

    Moonji Publishing, 2025

    Selected for Daesan Literary Grant · Book website: https://flo.host/2fBloO4/

    Examines how literary authorship and narrative forms transform in an era when artificial intelligence writes fiction. Spanning discussions on generative AI, AI-generated literature, science fiction, and posthumanism, this critical study questions human creativity and the future of literature.

  • Narratology of Embodied Cognition

    Pagijong Press, 2023

    Selected as an Outstanding Academic Book by the National Academy of Sciences of Korea

    Explores the relationship between bodily sensations, cognition, and narrative forms in Korean fiction dealing with illness and healing. From the perspective of cognitive narratology, it investigates how the experience of an ill body acquires meaning in literature and expands into a way of understanding others and the world.
